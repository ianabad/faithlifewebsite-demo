Privacy & cookies.

How information is handled when you visit this website or contact our church.

Who to contact

Faith Life Community Ministries, 427 Matheson Avenue, Winnipeg, Manitoba R2V 3A7, Canada, operates this church website. For a privacy question, an information request, or a concern about a photograph, contact our church leadership team through the contact page or write to the address above, marked “Privacy”.

Information you choose to share

The contact form asks for your name, email address, subject and message. Your phone number is optional. We use this information to respond to your request and arrange any follow-up you ask for. Sending an inquiry does not subscribe you to a mailing list.

Share only what is needed for your inquiry. Please avoid sending financial details, identification documents, medical information, confidential counselling details, or private information about someone else. Ask our team about a suitable way to discuss sensitive matters.

The form submits to the church’s existing WordPress contact service at flcmonline.com. That service and its email and spam-protection providers may process your submission. The form does not save your message in this static website or its public source repository.

Hosting and routine technical information

This version of the website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Hosting providers may also process routine request information needed to deliver and protect the website. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

This version does not include advertising pixels or first-party audience analytics. Ordinary links to other websites do not load their pages until you follow the link.

Optional players, maps and browser storage

Spotify players and Google Maps embeds are blocked until you choose to load them or enable that provider below. Loading an embed connects your browser to that provider, which may receive your IP address, browser information and activity within the embed, and may use cookies or similar technologies under its own policies.

You can use the rest of the website without enabling these services. Read Spotify’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy before enabling an embed. Opening a link directly to a provider also takes you to its service.

If you save preferences, this site stores only your provider choices, the preference version and a timestamp in your browser’s local storage for up to six months. This is not an advertising identifier. A “Load once” button applies only to that embed on the current page and does not save permission for future visits.

Turn a provider off here to prevent future embedded loads. This cannot erase information already received by that provider or remove cookies it has set. Manage those cookies through your browser or the provider. If browser storage is unavailable, your saved choice applies only to the current page.

Your privacy choices

Both optional services are off by default. Enable only the services you want. These choices apply to this browser.

Optional external content

Access, sharing and retention

Information sent to the church is for the team members and service providers who need it to handle your request. We do not sell inquiry information or use it for advertising. Information may also need to be disclosed where required by law or to address a serious safety concern.

We keep inquiry information only as long as needed for its purpose and any applicable recordkeeping obligations. Contact our team to ask about retention, access, correction, deletion, withdrawing consent, or raising a privacy concern. We will consider your request under the requirements that apply and explain any limits, such as records we must retain. Withdrawing consent may limit our ability to continue responding to a request.

Hosting, email and embedded-content providers may process information outside Canada, where local laws may allow access by public authorities. Electronic messages cannot be guaranteed confidential. If a matter is particularly sensitive, contact our team to discuss another way to communicate.

Children and photographs

A parent or guardian should contact us on behalf of a young child. Please avoid including a child’s private information in a general website message. If you have a concern about a photograph of yourself or your child, identify the page and image through our contact page so our team can review it.

Visitors outside Canada and changes

This website serves a church in Winnipeg, Canada. If you visit from elsewhere, your information may be handled in Canada and in countries used by the providers described above. You can ask our team about privacy rights that apply in your circumstances. We will update this page when the website’s data practices change. The date below identifies the latest revision.
