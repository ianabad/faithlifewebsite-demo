# Website privacy and accessibility handover

Updated November 6, 2026. Applies to the GitHub Pages version of Faith Life Community Ministries' website.

## Implemented

- Footer links on every page to privacy/cookies, privacy choices, accessibility and website terms.
- Spotify and Google Maps disconnected by default. Each may be enabled separately, or loaded once without saving consent.
- Saved provider preferences expire after 183 days. No location lookup or advertising/audience analytics added.
- Contact form notice, optional telephone field, autocomplete labels and required consent to handling an inquiry. No newsletter subscription implied.
- Accessibility feedback and alternate-format request route through the contact page and postal address.
- Keyboard focus styling and reduced-motion overrides. Announcement details remain available as text.

## Actions for church leadership

1. Assign a person to handle privacy and accessibility requests and monitor the contact channel. The public notice currently routes requests to the church leadership team; it does not invent an officer or email address.
2. Confirm the existing WordPress contact form at flcmonline.com delivers correctly. No test inquiry was sent. Review its email host, spam services, storage, access permissions, retention and any cross-border processing. The static-site notice describes this handler; changing it requires updating the notice.
3. Document and apply inquiry retention/deletion rules. Do not retain information indefinitely without a purpose. Review access, correction, deletion and privacy-incident procedures with appropriate advice.
4. Confirm consent/permissions for identifiable church photographs, especially children, and permissions for artwork and recordings. Posting terms does not obtain photo consent or cure copyright issues.
5. Arrange a full WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility audit and address findings. This change is not a conformance certification. Provide suitable accessible alternatives and train staff/volunteers as required.
6. Obtain Manitoba legal review of the policy wording and actual church practices. Nonprofit status alone does not determine all privacy obligations. Review other jurisdictions if the church intentionally targets or monitors people there.
7. If adding analytics, ad pixels, newsletters, registrations, donations or a new host, review consent, CASL where applicable, provider contracts and these notices before release. Do not wire additional providers into the site ahead of consent.
